## 1. Volume and mix

- The Ministry (ÇED, İzin ve Denetim GM) publishes ÇED statistics by year and province: "ÇED olumlu" and "ÇED gerekli değildir" counts, 1993–2025, page updated 02.03.2026 https://ced.csb.gov.tr/ced-istatistikleri-111186. The tables themselves were not readable through search; the Aegean-province share therefore could not be computed (gap).
- Long-run scale: 1993–2010 saw 33,824 decisions, of which 32 "olumsuz" and 31,285 "gerekli değildir" https://tr.boell.org/tr/2014/06/16/ced-raporlari-cevresel-etkiyi-degerlendirebilir-mi-yayinlar. 2002–end 2023: 72,295 "ÇED gerekli değildir" decisions, 47% of them petroleum/mining, 14% agriculture-chemistry, 13% industry, 9% waste-chemistry https://medyascope.tv/2024/03/11/resmi-verilerle-cevre-bakanliginin-ced-tablosu-akp-doneminde-72-bin-kez-ced-gerekli-degildir-karari-verildi/ . For 1993–2023, 7,437 "ÇED olumlu" decisions; roughly 28% petroleum/mining and 23% energy (same search result set, attribution to likely but not certain).
- 2025 (Bakanlık data as reported): 4,674 decisions; 716 olumlu, 3,056 gerekli değildir, 304 olumsuz; energy = 422 olumlu and 395 gerekli değildir https://t24.com.tr/cevre/bakanligin-raporu-ortaya-koydu-binlerce-projeye-cevre-onayi-verildi,1304795 (also https://dokuz8haber.net/bakanlik-raporu-binlerce-projeye-cevre-onayi-verildi). Caveat: 716+3,056+304 = 4,076, not 4,674; the year and arithmetic need checking against. If correct, energy would be about 59% of olumlu decisions in 2025 (422/716; own arithmetic).
- 2026 to 29 May: 1,366 projects acted on, 307 olumlu, 1,219 "gerekli değildir", 92 RES projects approved https://www.cumhuriyet.com.tr/turkiye/bakanlik-yeni-yila-hizli-basladi-2026-nin-ortasinda-projelerin-verileri-dikkat-cekti-2507875 (counts do not add up; treat as indicative).
- RES/GES/JES/HES split by technology: not found as a Ministry table. A monthly third-party digest of energy ÇED results exists https://insaattedarik.com.tr/enerji/enerji-projeleri-ced-sonuclari-temmuz-2025/ and https://www.polenekoloji.org/bakanliktan-yeni-ced-izinleri-temmuzda-suruyor/ — could be scraped to build the split.
- Aegean geography: installed RES by province (July 2019) was highest in İzmir (1,886 MW), Balıkesir (1,375 MW), Manisa (727.55 MW); Çanakkale 917.35 MW https://www.gmka.gov.tr/haber/ruzgar-enerjisinde-lider-sehirler-balikesir-ve-canakkale and https://ritm.enerji.gov.tr/news/res.php (the search summary's ordering of Çanakkale and Manisa is inconsistent). Aydın: 9 olumlu decisions (solar, wind, geothermal, mining, industry) between 20.02.2025 and 03.06.2026 https://www.aydintimes.com/aydinda-6-ilceye-ced-vizesi-9-proje-onaylandi . Indicates the Aegean/Marmara belt is dense in wind, but no sourced provincial share of ÇED decisions.

Regulation
- ÇED Yönetmeliği of 29.07.2022 (RG 31907): Ek-1 projects all subject to full ÇED; thresholds for tourism, wind/solar and industry revised https://www.mondaq.com/turkey/climate-change/1644674/%C3%87evresel-etki-de%C4%9Ferlendirmesi-y%C3%B6netmeli%C4%9Finde-%C3%96nemli-de%C4%9Fi%C5%9Fiklikler ; formats Ek-3/Ek-4 https://www.cevremuhendisligi.org/index.php/cevre-aktuel/haberler/2124-ced-yonetmeligi-formati-hakkinda .
- Amendment of 05.03.2026 (RG 33187): "ÇED gerekli değildir" removed; decision types become olumlu / olumsuz / "ÇED raporu hazırlanmalıdır"; report due within 1 year (+180 days once); olumlu lapses if investment does not start within 5 years https://www.alomaliye.com/2026/03/05/ced-yonetmeligi-degisti-ced-gerekli-degildir-kaldirildi-ced-raporu-hazirlanmalidir-donemi-basladi/ ; also https://www.mondaq.com/turkey/environmental-law/1767728/ and https://www.lbfpartners.com/haberler-yayinlar/ced-yonetmeligi-nde-koklu-degisiklikler-baslica-yenilikler-ve-uygulamaya-yansimalari . May increase the number of full reports and so the number of biodiversity sections required (analyst inference; no sourced volume forecast).
- Ek-5 sensitive areas widened: drinking-water basins, Bern Convention areas, Mediterranean monk seal habitats https://emlakkulisi.com/guncel/ced-yonetmeligi-duyarli-yoreler/622747 .
- Wetland Regulation amended 05.09.2026: renewable energy allowed in sustainable-use zones of designated wetlands, environmental-flow requirements, cumulative impact report for industrial zones in controlled-use areas https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2026/09/20260905-24.htm ; https://yesilhaber.net/suni-sulak-alanlar-enerji-izni-cevresel-akis/ .
- Nature law: the "Tabiatı ve Biyolojik Çeşitliliği Koruma Kanunu" draft has been tabled three times since 2002 without passing; a fourth attempt reported https://www.tarimhukuku.org/biyolojik-cesitliligi-koruma-kanunu-4-kez-meclise-gelecek/ ; NGO opposition https://www.wwf.org.tr/?1354%2Fdogakorumayayasayladarbe= . A separate Milli Parklar law proposal passed committee, giving DKMP authority over wetland/threatened species oversight https://www.ajans344.com/gundem/milli-parklar-ve-doga-korumada-yeni-donem-kanun-teklifi-tbmmde-kabul-edildi/400479 (status of enactment not confirmed).
- DKMP requirements in the ÇED process: DKMP instruction of 27.06.2011 (no. 59352) set a special "Ekosistem Değerlendirme Raporu" format for all RES and mining projects; 15.03.2011 (no. 21767) required an assessment report for hydraulic activity for HES; DKMP approves the report and it enters the ÇED annex https://uluscevre.com.tr/hizmet/ced-cevresel-etki-degerlendirme/ekosistem-degerlendirme-raporu (vendor page citing the instruction; primary DKMP text not seen). Ornithological Evaluation Reports (ODR) are requested by DKMP for RES/GES https://www.enpark.com.tr/hizmetler/ornitolojik-degerlendirme-raporu/ . A formal DKMP RES bird/bat guidance document was not found in search (unsourced).

Whether authors are in-house biologists, academics or freelancers is not documented in a public source (unsourced; consistent with the court expert panels in section 5 being university-based).

## 3. Tools and data in use

- Nuh'un Gemisi national biodiversity database (DKMP): vascular plants, mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians, freshwater fish; queries by province; about 1.8–2 million records https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/DKMP/Haber/57/Yeni-Nuhun-Gemisi-Ulusal-Biyolojik-Cesitlilik-Veri-Tabani-Halka-Acildi and https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/ekonomi/ulusal-biyolojik-cesitlilik-veri-tabanindaki-veri-sayisi-2-milyona-yaklasti/3226919 . Use of TÜBİVES, GBIF, CORINE, QGIS/ArcGIS inside ÇED fieldwork was not confirmed by a source (unsourced; expected but not evidenced).
- Field methods in Turkish wind-farm biodiversity work: fixed-point bird observation, transects, passive ultrasonic bat detectors, one-year monitoring for flora, birds, bats, mammals, herpetofauna https://www3.dfc.gov/environment/eia/enerjisa/9-IhlamurWPP/Ihlamur%20RES_TOO.pdf ; acoustic bat monitoring studies exist in Türkiye https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/commagene/article/1680176 . eDNA in Turkish EIAs: none found. Camera traps: listed by consultancies.
- Global screening layers: update of the global Critical Habitat screening layer https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC12627699/ . IBAT use in Türkiye not evidenced in this scan (R4 covers IBAT).
- Turkish software/startups for ÇED or biodiversity screening: none found (one search; unsourced "none").

## 4. Lender-driven demand

- Enerjisa Üretim's nine-project wind package (750 MW, 180 turbines, Aegean and Marmara) went through ESIA, stand-alone Critical Habitat Assessments (CHA) and a Framework Biodiversity Action Plan under IFC PS6; CHAs for Hacıhıdırlar, Harmancık, Ihlamur, Uygar, Kestanederesi, Akköy are public on DFC https://www3.dfc.gov/environment/eia/enerjisa/9-IhlamurWPP/Ihlamur%20WPP_CHA_20240614_clean.pdf ; Ihlamur priority features: 6 birds, 3 plants, 13 mammals, 1 reptile. Framework BAP: https://www.dfc.gov/sites/default/files/esia/2024/enerjisa/11-Project_wide_documents/Enerjisa_YEKA9_BAPFramework_Final.pdf. Projects run one-year baseline monitoring to fill data gaps and update the CHA (herpetofauna included).
- EBRD side: Enerjisa wind (project 54230 and 54892, with Falp, Gaia, Arturna BMPs, ESR6) https://www.ebrd.com/home/work-with-us/projects/psd/54230.html ; SUEZ wind interim BAP https://www.ebrd.com/content/dam/ebrd_dxp/documents/project/55457/55457-updated-bap.pdf ; other Turkish BMPs https://www.ebrd.com/content/dam/ebrd_dxp/documents/project/56056/56056_BMP_EN.pdf and https://www.ebrd.com/content/dam/ebrd_dxp/documents/project/55854/55854-bmp.pdf. Consultants named so far: Mott MacDonald and ERM. Pattern: 2 consultancies for a 9–10 project portfolio, i.e. the lender-grade market is concentrated and portfolio-based.
- Turkish banks: TSKB scores projects with its ERET model aligned to the Equator Principles and excludes projects in protected areas or critical habitat unless adequate mitigation is possible; has a Nature and Biodiversity Policy https://www.tskb.com.tr/uploads/file/tskb-sustainable-finance-framework-2024.pdf and https://www.tskb.com.tr/tskbentegrerapor2025/tskb-integrated-annual-report-2025.html . Garanti BBVA reported as the first Turkish Equator Principles bank and issuer of a biodiversity blue bond https://gfmag.com/sustainable-finance/garanti-bbva-the-next-frontier-of-sustainable-finance-in-turkey/ . İş Bankası and Akbank specific biodiversity requirements: not found.
- Turkish IFC PS6 text available: https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/2010/2012-ifc-performance-standard-6-tr.pdf.

## 5. Gaps

- Court record: the "ÇED olumlu" for Sabancı's Gaia RES (Muğla) was annulled after the court found flora and bird monitoring insufficient; a new olumlu was issued on 24.09.2026 after about 1.5 months of re-processing, with turbines already erected https://www.egedesonsoz.com/turbinler-kuruldu-iptal-geldi-sabancinin-resi-yeniden-masada and https://www.egedesonsoz.com/danistaydan-donmustu-sabancinin-projesine-bakanlik-onayi . Indicates the legal and financial cost of a weak biodiversity baseline, and that remedy is fast once data exist.
- Court expert panel (Aydın 2. İdare Mahkemesi, Germencik case): ÇED listed 6 amphibian and 6 reptile species; literature indicated substantially more were possible in the area https://akinyakan.av.tr/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/GERMENCIK-BILIRKISI-RAPORU-1.pdf . Direct evidence of the herpetofauna gap.
- Other judicial examples: fauna assessment omitting invertebrates and bees, incomplete forest-fire plan (Danıştay/ÇED olumlu cancellation) https://www.mansetiz.com/91-bin-agac-icin-kritik-donemec-danistay-ced-olumlu-iptal-kararini-bozdu ; Danıştay reversals both ways, so outcome risk is two-sided https://halktv.com.tr/gundem/mahkemenin-ced-iptalini-danistay-iptal-etti-1055728h .
- Academic critique: observation periods are very short; fauna and flora visits on the same dates; species lists reuse the same sources; key species missing in Foça, Karabiga, Yeşilovacık reports https://sadafag.org/ced-raporlari-ve-ekosistemlerin-korunmasi-arasindaki-iliski/ .
- Wind-farm specific: a Kütahya 18-turbine RES file reported all turbine locations as a collision risk for bats, with unspecified tree-felling numbers https://www.cumhuriyet.com.tr/turkiye/res-raporunda-kus-ve-yarasalar-icin-carpisma-riski-vurgusu-ekolojik-alarm-uyarisi-2538306 .
- Odonata: no source found on Odonata omissions in ÇED (unsourced; likely absent from standard lists, which is the differentiator hypothesis, not a finding).
- Cumulative impact: new wetland regulation requires cumulative impact reports for industrial areas; for wind clusters in Aegean provinces no sourced cumulative framework found.

### Where a sourced pre-screening product could help (hypotheses, not findings)
1. Consultancies: a polygon-in species-likelihood table with Nuh'un Gemisi/literature/GBIF provenance could shorten desk-phase work and give a citable record when an expert panel later lists "possible species" (as in Germencik).
2. Developers: pre-screen before land lease/YEKA bidding for Ek-5 sensitive areas and likely PS6 critical-habitat triggers.
3. Lenders (TSKB, Garanti BBVA, others): a first-pass CHA screen for E&S due diligence, with expert verification flagged.
4. Litigation-facing users (municipalities, NGOs, law firms): sourced gap lists against filed ÇED species lists.

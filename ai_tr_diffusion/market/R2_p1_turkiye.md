# R2 — P1 Türkiye + Mediterranean demand for field-labelled wetland/biodiversity data

Date stamp: 2026-10-05. Method: 40 WebSearch calls (several calls returned the results of 2–4 internal sub-queries) + 2 Consensus queries. Fetch is blocked, so every URL below was seen in a search result ("URL via search result"); many facts rest on the search engine's summary of a page, not on a full read. Where attribution of a number to a specific page is uncertain this is stated. Language is calibrated: "suggests", "may". Source list with IDs: `R2_sources.csv`.

Bottom line: the search found policy and grant demand signals (national data library, wetland inventory, Biodiversa+ funding, DKMP-commissioned inventories), but no evidence of any purchase, licence or tender for labelled EO/bioacoustic training data in Türkiye. Existing demand looks like commissioned field surveys and research grants, not data licensing.

## 1. Turkish public demand

**1a. 2026–2030 Yapay Zekâ Eylem Planı and data library**
- The plan was reported as announced by the President in June 2026 and published as Cumhurbaşkanlığı Genelgesi 2026/9 in Resmî Gazete 33344 on 18 Aug 2026; coordinated by the Sanayi ve Teknoloji Bakanlığı. [https://yzo.klu.edu.tr/Sayfalar/45371-turkiye-2026-2030-yapay-zeka-eylem-plani-aciklandi.klu] [https://webrazzi.com/2026/08/18/turkiye-nin-2026-2030-yapay-zek-eylem-plani-yururluge-girdi/] [https://www.setav.org/teknokutup-cagda-turkiyenin-yapay-zeka-eylem-plani-2026-2030]
- Data target: at least 2,000 public datasets in the Ulusal Veri Kütüphanesi in machine-readable form, at least 100 of them via regular API access; named sectors are health, agriculture, defence and e-commerce. [https://yzo.klu.edu.tr/Sayfalar/45371-turkiye-2026-2030-yapay-zeka-eylem-plani-aciklandi.klu]
- A search summary states the plan aims to create datasets in "health, agriculture, environment and industry" for researchers and developers. The exact action line, owner institution and any "etiketli veri seti" (labelled dataset) wording were not found; the official action table was not retrieved. [https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/analiz/turkiye-nin-dijital-egemenlik-rotasinda-2026-2030-yapay-zeka-eylem-plani/4031396] [https://yztd.org.tr/tr/yayinlar/turkiye-yapay-zeka-eylem-plani]
- Inference (may): the library target is about publishing public data, not buying external data; a biodiversity/wetland benchmark would fit only as a donated/open contribution unless a funded action line exists.

**1b. TÜBİTAK UZAY / Türksat EO programmes**
- TÜBİTAK UZAY builds EO satellites (RASAT, Göktürk-2, İMECE; İMECE launched 15 Apr 2023, sub-metre PAN, 3.96 m RGB-NIR). [https://uzay.tubitak.gov.tr/en/imece/]
- The GEZGİN portal was built to share RASAT imagery online (via search summary). [https://webdosya.csb.gov.tr/db/cbsgunu/webmenu/webmenu15787.pdf]
- A "national remote sensing data integrated management" committee report exists (TUCBS, 2025); content not read. [https://tucbs.gov.tr/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/UUAV_ulusal_zaktan_alg_lama_verileri_heyet_raporu-Platform.pdf]
- No Türksat EO-data programme, no TÜBİTAK UZAY biodiversity/wetland validation call and no purchase of external ground-truth data was found.

**1c. DKMP Nuh'un Gemisi Ulusal Biyolojik Çeşitlilik Veri Tabanı**
- Holds vascular plants, mammals, birds, reptiles, amphibians and inland-water fish; web-based, publicly queryable; run by DKMP under UBENİS (Ulusal Biyolojik Çeşitlilik Envanter ve İzleme Projesi). [https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/DKMP/Haber/57/Yeni-Nuhun-Gemisi-Ulusal-Biyolojik-Cesitlilik-Veri-Tabani-Halka-Acildi]
- Size: reported as approaching 2 million records; earlier reports gave 1.43–1.5 million; UBENİS field monitoring was reported at 852,644 coordinated points covering 13,409 plant and animal species. [https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/ekonomi/ulusal-biyolojik-cesitlilik-veri-tabanindaki-veri-sayisi-2-milyona-yaklasti/3226919] [https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/yasam/nuhun-gemisine-1-5-milyon-veri-yuklendi/1084850] [https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/bilim-teknoloji/turkiyenin-biyolojik-cesitliligine-853-bin-noktadan-yakin-takip/1768717]
- Does it accept or buy external data? Not found. Data enter through DKMP-commissioned provincial inventories procured by open tender (e.g. Muğla, 540 days; Ankara, 730 days, DKMP 9. Bölge, IKN 2026/1788766, tender date 16 Oct 2026). Contract amounts were not shown in the search results. [https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/DKMP/Sayfalar/Detay.aspx?OgeId=93&Liste=Ihaleler] [https://www.ticarihayat.com/ankara-ilinin-karasal-ve-ic-su-ekosistemleri-biyolojik-cesitlilik-envanter-ve-izleme-isi]
- Records are point occurrences (taxon observations); no evidence of image, audio or EO-label content, so it is a complement, not a substitute.

**1d. Wetland inventory and Ramsar monitoring**
- SAYBİS (Ulusal Sulak Alan Envanter ve Yönetimi Bilgi Sistemi) has a public interface and a staff data-entry interface. [https://saybis.tarimorman.gov.tr/]
- The Minister stated that 6,418 wetlands totalling 1.6 million ha were recorded in SAYBİS and the national inventory is near completion. [https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/yesilhat/dogal-yasam/6-bin-418-sulak-alan-kayit-altina-alindi/1822315]
- Registered sites: 14 Ramsar (184,487 ha), 59 national-importance, 27 local-importance; 100 registered wetlands, 1,095,904 ha (via search summary). [https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/DKMP/Menu/31/Sulak-Alanlar]
- Management plans: 70 wetland management plans were prepared 1999–2021; a search summary gives 6,926,000 TL spent on "Sulak Alan Envanter, Yönetim Planı ve İzleme Projesi" work since 2013. The page this figure comes from was not isolated (candidates below); treat as unverified. [https://www.aa.com.tr/tr/cevre/turkiyedeki-sulak-alanlari-koruma-seferberligi/2541221] [https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/gundem/sulak-alanlar-icin-bes-yillik-plan-38541466]
- Regulation: the Sulak Alanların Korunması Yönetmeliği requires annual monitoring reports to local commissions in protected zones. [https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2014/04/20140404-11.htm]
- Ministry statements (via search summary) say satellite remote sensing was integrated with AI for crop-pattern/agricultural land detection; not wetland-specific. [https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/SGB/Belgeler/stratejikplan.pdf]
- Academic EO monitoring of Turkish Ramsar sites exists (see 3a) but is research-led, not an operational ministry programme that was found.

**1e. TÜBİTAK calls**
- 1071 (Biodiversa+ national funding): TÜBİTAK is a Biodiversa+ partner; Turkish applicants to BiodivMon (2022–23) and later calls must also apply nationally to 1071. [https://tubitak.gov.tr/en/node/11947]
- Biodiversa+ 2025 BiodivConnect (restoration): scope includes wetlands; TÜBİTAK reserved EUR 350,000 (via search summary); cap per project EUR 160,000, EUR 100,000 per higher-education/public institution. [https://tubitak.gov.tr/en/announcement/biodiversa-2025-call-restoration-ecosystem-functioning-integrity-and-connectivity-biodivconnect-open-application] [https://www.biodiversa.eu/biodivconnect-funding-organisations/] [https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2025-09/Ulusal_Basvuru_Kosullari.pdf]
- 1001: ceiling reported as 3,000,000 TRY from the first 2026 period (via search summary of a guide whose file path is dated 2024; verify). [https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2024-04/ardeb_1001_basvuru_rehberi.pdf]
- 1007 (Kamu Kurumları AR-GE, KAMAG): a public institution acts as "customer institution" and commits to adopt results; need areas come from KAMAG focus groups valid 3 years. A 2026 call document exists (1007-SGB-2026-01), content not read. No 1007 call naming wetland/biodiversity EO validation was found. [https://tubitak.gov.tr/en/funds/akademik/ulusal-destek-programlari/1007-public-institutions-research-and-development-projects-support-program] [https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2026-03/1007-SGB-2026-01_0.pdf]
- 2244 (industry doctorate): not searched specifically; no information.
- A search summary mentions a Yeşil Sanayi R&D topic list containing an "ecosystem and biodiversity observation networks" topic; attribution to the specific document unverified. [https://tubitak.gov.tr/sites/default/files/2024-03/db-yesil-ar-ge_ve_yenilik_konu_basliklari-v7_1.pdf]

## 2. Mediterranean

**2a. Tour du Valat / Mediterranean Wetlands Observatory (MWO)**
- MWO was created in 2008 at the request of the MedWet Committee as a multi-partner project coordinated by Tour du Valat; it works at regional, national and local levels with MedWet member countries (Türkiye is a member). [https://medwet.org/medwet/mediterranean-wetland-observatory/]
- MWO-3 ("Responses to ongoing crises"), published for Ramsar COP15 (July 2025), uses 18 DPSIR indicators with partners across 28 MedWet countries; over 56% of natural wetlands are reported lost. A Mediterranean Wetlands Geoportal accompanies it. [https://tourduvalat.org/en/publications-en/mediterranean-wetlands-outlook-3-mwo-3/] [https://tourduvalat.org/en/newsletter-articles/mediterranean-wetlands-responses-to-ongoing-crises/] [https://medwet.org/save-the-date-webinar-for-the-mediterranean-wetland-outlook-3-and-the-mediterranean-wetlands-geoportal/]
- EO products: Tour du Valat uses GEOclassifier (from the SWOS project) for wetland mapping; products include Potential Wetland Areas and Potentially Restorable Wetlands maps (Sebou, Morocco; Medjerda, Algeria/Tunisia; >7,000 km2 and 1,700 km2 of lost wetland habitat identified). [https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41207-023-00443-6]
- Validation: compared with existing local inventories, reported reliability up to 95% in main wetland areas (indicator paper); Tour du Valat-associated work reports >86% of field-inventoried wetlands detected by the wetland probability map (via search summary). [https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4380444/] [https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41207-023-00443-6]
- Perennou et al. (2018) argue that Mediterranean wetland RS needs ecological knowledge and warns that good-looking maps can be wrong; small, seasonal and vegetated wetlands are hard cases. [https://consensus.app/papers/details/d0ef7dac2e2a5d58b7bd883264311dc3/?utm_source=claude_desktop]
- No Turkish national partner for MWO products was identified in the results; ESA GlobWetland-II used Menderes Delta and Güllük Bay as Turkish test sites. [https://consensus.app/papers/details/d737895838a757e4a9501b9748806c79/?utm_source=claude_desktop]
- Leberger et al. (2020): SE Mediterranean sites combine low protection with large land-cover change and lack biodiversity observation data. This supports a data-gap argument for Türkiye. [https://consensus.app/papers/details/306b1b00aa385106b1e051777c0fc240/?utm_source=claude_desktop]

**2b. Copernicus HRL Water & Wetness**
- Target thematic accuracies (2018 product): permanent water 85%, temporary water 80%, permanent wet 80%, temporary wet 80%, dry 85%; based on Sentinel-1/2. [https://land.copernicus.eu/en/products/high-resolution-layer-water-and-wetness/water-and-wetness-status-2018]
- A national quality assessment (Ireland, 2015 product) judged overall quality insufficient, with poor capture of peatland and temporary wet areas. This suggests independent in-situ validation has value, particularly for temporary/vegetated wetness. [https://gis.epa.ie/geonetwork/geonetwork/api/records/26ae46e4-a1d1-47b2-a5b0-52da004396fa]
- Method behind HRL W&W (Ludwig et al. 2019) is highly automated and needs little training data, reported >92% overall accuracy at three sites. [https://consensus.app/papers/details/3638c88ca10b520481b1beeb64bf478c/?utm_source=claude_desktop]

**2c. Biodiversa+ with Turkish partners**
- BiodivMon (2022–23): 33 projects funded, >EUR 46 million; one Turkish university researcher (Oğuz Türkozan, Aydın Adnan Menderes University) appeared in the BiodivMon material (via search summary; project not identified). [https://www.biodiversa.eu/2023/12/01/biodivmon-call-33-projects-funded/] [https://www.biodiversa.eu/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Biodiversa_2022-2023_BiodivMon_WEB_Light.pdf]
- TABMON (acoustic monitoring network) is one BiodivMon project, showing acoustic monitoring is a funded theme. [https://www.biodiversa.eu/2024/04/15/tabmon/]
- BiodivConnect (2025–26): 278 pre-proposals, 123 eligible full proposals, 36 projects funded, >EUR 50 million; the list of funded projects with Turkish partners was not found. [https://anr.fr/en/call-for-proposals-details/call/biodivconnect-restoration-of-ecosystem-functioning-integrity-and-connectivity-biodiversa-2025/]

## 3. Existing Turkish / Mediterranean labelled datasets

**3a. EO**
- No open, labelled Turkish wetland/land-cover benchmark (TÜBİTAK or university release) was found. Search returned many Turkish journal papers using Sentinel-2 classifiers with local points. [Search: Turkish land cover Sentinel-2 queries; no dataset URL found]
- Peer-reviewed Turkish wetland EO studies with their own reference data: Sakarbaşı (Sentinel-2, kappa 0.95); Balıkdamı (Sentinel-1+2 fusion, ~90% OA, compared with UAV data); Balıkdamı unmixing (30 cm aerial reference; extent precision 99.21%, abundance OA 66.17%); coastal Ramsar sites water-surface change with GEE (Gediz Delta water surface +46.0% 1985–2020); Göksu Delta (Lake Akgöl lost 58.85% of water body over 38 years). [https://consensus.app/papers/details/22983e3675035de4ac92a223f879c7b3/?utm_source=claude_desktop] [https://consensus.app/papers/details/7a4a3b1d76db57f08129e6fb3a7f6f35/?utm_source=claude_desktop] [https://consensus.app/papers/details/22ac966ee2e5546c977c85fc176911ed/?utm_source=claude_desktop] [https://consensus.app/papers/details/2e286ad9b1d2557fa372cb06ecec4536/?utm_source=claude_desktop] [https://consensus.app/papers/details/2228e700e1b459208c36ac36d593b1c0/?utm_source=claude_desktop]
- Mediterranean label-scarcity evidence: a 2026 Morocco study reports 20–30 local labels per class recover ~96–98% of full-data performance; Rapinel et al. validated a French national wetland map with ~3,000 field plots; UAV plus floristic surveys serve as habitat reference. [https://consensus.app/papers/details/a6940475ced35bfd8a0c50fd7a07f74f/?utm_source=claude_desktop] [https://consensus.app/papers/details/422bd06d26e65ded9c2566134ec42cb0/?utm_source=claude_desktop] [https://consensus.app/papers/details/af1e16f65ec85d9a88cb64642db71234/?utm_source=claude_desktop]

**3b. Bioacoustics**
- Aras bird-sound study (JIST): 22 species from Iğdır Aras River Bird Paradise, 10 s clips to 1 s log-mel spectrograms, CNN/LSTM and transfer learning (VGGish, YAMNet); best accuracy 94.2%, F1 92.8% (VGGish). Recordings appear to come from public archives; no release of the dataset was confirmed. [https://dergipark.org.tr/tr/pub/jist/article/1124674]
- Another Turkish study targets Kızılgerdan (robin) recordings. [https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/dubited/issue/55096/569642]
- No Turkish anuran acoustic dataset or YÖK thesis on anuran call classification was found in two searches (Turkish and English). This suggests a real gap, with the caveat that YÖK Tez was not searchable via this tool.

**3c. Occurrence/image data**
- Amphibians of Türkiye: a Scientific Data 2024 database has 5,611 occurrence records for 37 species from 436 literature sources. [https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-024-03101-w]
- GBIF Türkiye counts per taxon were not retrievable (fetch blocked; searches returned only generic pages). iNaturalist has a Türkiye place page and an "Odonata of Türkiye" project; observation counts were not retrieved. [https://www.inaturalist.org/places/turkiye]
- DNA barcoding: reference barcodes exist for Turkish fish; search summaries suggest BOLD is thinly populated for Turkish species; no "TürBOL" initiative was found. [https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/1755-0998.12120]

## 5. Gaps and regulatory hooks

- CBD/GBF: Türkiye's NBSAP (UBSEP) covers 2018–2028 and must be revised against the Kunming-Montreal GBF; NGO documents call for an update. A finalised 7th National Report and updated NBSAP indicators were not found, so whether TR indicators need EO-derived wetland data is unestablished. [https://www.wwf.org.tr/?14660%2FBiyolojikCesitlilikUlusalStratejisiveEylemPlan=] [https://wwftr.awsassets.panda.org/downloads/ubsep-22052024.pdf]
- Ulusal Sulak Alan Stratejisi: the wetlands regulation tasks the National Wetland Commission with national wetland policy and strategy; a search summary says a 10-year strategy aligned with the Ramsar Strategic Plan will be prepared. No strategy text with targets or EO requirements was found. [https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2014/04/20140404-11.htm] [https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/SGB/Belgeler/stratejikplan.pdf]
- TSRS (ISSB-aligned) is mandatory from 1 Jan 2024 for in-scope entities exceeding thresholds; no TSRS standard specific to nature was found. TNFD uptake in Türkiye is limited: TKYB (development bank) is reported as a TNFD adopter; CarrefourSA joined the TNFD Forum; Sabancı, Arçelik and Akenerji report TNFD-style screening. This suggests nature-data demand is nascent and corporate. [https://kgk.gov.tr/Portalv2Uploads/files/Duyurular/v2/Surdurulebilirlik/Duyuru/TSRS%20Uygulama%20Kapsam%C4%B1na%20%C4%B0li%C5%9Fkin%20Kurul%20Kararlar%C4%B1na%20Dair%20Usul%20ve%20Esaslar.pdf] [https://kalkinma.com.tr/uploads/file/tkyb-entegre-rapor-2025.pdf] [https://akenerji.com.tr/Dosya/Dokuman/Iklim%20ve%20Doga%20Bag%CC%86lant%C4%B1l%C4%B1%20Risk%20ve%20Firsatlar%20Raporu_%20TNFD-TCFD.PDF] [https://www.egetelgraf.com/carrefoursa-tnfd-forumuna-katildi] [https://surdurulebilirlik.sabanci.com/2023/doga-gundemimiz.aspx]

[https://www.ab.gov.tr/deniz-habitatlarinin-ve-potansiyel-deniz-natura-2000-alanlarinin-belirlenmesi_53987.html] [https://www.tarimorman.gov.tr/DKMP/Haber/40/Natura-2000-Gerekliliklerinin-Uygulanabilmesi-Icin-Ulusal-Doga-Koruma-Sisteminin-Guclendirilmesi-Projesi-Kapanis-Toplantisi-Gerceklestirildi%E2%80%A6]

## Gaps

1. No Turkish open labelled wetland EO benchmark found; existing studies use bespoke points. A multi-site benchmark with habitat labels would be novel here (caveat: search tool limits).

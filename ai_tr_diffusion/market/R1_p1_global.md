# R1 - P1 global: market for field-labelled EO and bioacoustic validation/training data

Workstream R1, product P1 (Gediz Delta Mediterranean wetland benchmark). Date-stamped 2026-10-05.
Method: 40 WebSearch calls plus 5 Consensus queries (abstract level only; no full papers read). Outbound fetch is blocked, so every URL was seen in a search or Consensus result ("via search result"). Citation counts are Consensus snapshots on 2026-10-05. Where a fact was not found it says "not found". Every link label R1-nnn maps to `R1_sources.csv`.
Correction to the brief: SpeciesNet is a Google model (wildlife image classifier), not a Microsoft one; Microsoft's AI for Good Lab maintains MegaDetector [R1-059](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/03/google-releases-speciesnet-an-ai-model-designed-to-identify-wildlife/) [R1-058](https://github.com/microsoft/MegaDetector/tree/main).

## Summary: five most decision-relevant findings

1. The big EO and bioacoustic benchmarks are open, academic or institution-published and licensed permissively (CDLA-Permissive, CC-BY, CC0). The evidence suggests foundation-model teams consume them for free as evaluation sets; I found no instance of an FM team paying for labelled data. A paid-licence-only P1 therefore looks hard; open benchmark first, with a commercial licence as an add-on, matches the observed norm [R1-011](https://www.tensorflow.org/datasets/catalog/bigearthnet) [R1-013](https://huggingface.co/datasets/harshinde/sen1floods11) [R1-030](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02666-2) [R1-006](https://consensus.app/papers/details/7925bb243326541fb6732abfdf0e3f44/).
2. The only buyers of field ground truth with money that I found are forest-carbon rating/MRV firms (Sylvera, BeZero, Chloris, Planet, Pachama, Space Intelligence, Kanop). They invest heavily in LiDAR/plot data (Sylvera states more than USD 10M and 250,000 ha) and swap plot data for biomass maps (BeZero-Chloris), but all evidence is about forest biomass, not wetlands or fauna [R1-082](https://www.sylvera.com/products/forest-biomass-data) [R1-079](https://bezerocarbon.com/insights/bezero-carbon-partners-with-chloris-geospatial-to-strengthen-global-forest-carbon-measurement).
3. Foundation-model teams state that labels are the scarce input and that models generalise unevenly: AlphaEarth's abstract says high-quality labels remain scarce; PANGAEA reports benchmarks biased to North America and Europe and that GFMs do not consistently beat supervised baselines; EarthShift reports 15-20% worse out-of-distribution performance. This supports regional validation data but is not a purchase signal [R1-047](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.22291) [R1-004](https://consensus.app/papers/details/c9ba28e94dd65684b5faeb66aa594a86/) [R1-107](https://consensus.app/papers/details/a2f633807ef450d3bcd42b354c92ed7a/).
4. The wetland and herpetofauna gaps are real in the searches done: WorldCover reports lower accuracy for herbaceous wetland; aquatic vegetation is the hardest class to transfer between Mediterranean-type sites; BirdSet is bird-only; AnuraSet is Neotropical; anuran reviews call for large standardised anuran datasets; BigEarthNet covers ten countries, none Mediterranean-eastern or Turkish [R1-015](https://worldcover2021.esa.int/data/docs/WorldCover_PVR_V2.0.pdf) [R1-104](https://consensus.app/papers/details/fe4256f2f79158b388e4d2577cfe46ea/) [R1-027](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.10380) [R1-030](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02666-2) [R1-100](https://consensus.app/papers/details/f69b334b0d655d36a36419362d44fd6a/) [R1-008](https://bigearth.net/static/documents/Description_BigEarthNet-MM.pdf).
5. Price signals for labelled EO/ecology data are weak: no per-hectare or per-clip price was found. Adjacent signals: Planet subscriptions USD 1,100-12,000/yr, annotators USD 30-50/h, audio annotation USD 0.10-10/min, one geospatial dataset on AWS Marketplace at USD 150,000/12 months, NatureMetrics kits GBP 335 each [R1-068](https://www.planet.com/pricing/) [R1-074](https://www.opentrain.ai/solutions/satellite-imagery-annotation) [R1-075](https://kili-technology.com/data-labeling/data-labeling-services-price-q3-2023-benchmark) [R1-078](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-mzfuul6m4bxh6) [R1-089](https://www.naturemetrics.shop/).

- GBIF: accepts CC0, CC BY, CC BY-NC; >50% of records CC0 and >82% CC0 or CC BY; 83% of dataset publishers choose CC BY [R1-065](https://data-blog.gbif.org/post/gbif-occurrence-license-processing/) [R1-066](https://peterdesmet.com/posts/analyzing-gbif-data-licenses.html). - Licence norms seen: CC0 and CC-BY for research datasets (AnuraSet, Sen1Floods11, WABAD, AlphaEarth embeddings); CDLA-Permissive for BigEarthNet; CC-BY-NC with a commercial contact route for models (BirdNET) and imagery (Maxar); per-recording CC with ND exclusions for xeno-canto-derived sets [R1-030](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02666-2) [R1-013](https://huggingface.co/datasets/harshinde/sen1floods11) [R1-099](https://consensus.app/papers/details/c099d9b67a1d52f7af939eef0a36c1d5/) [R1-048](https://medium.com/google-earth/ai-powered-pixels-introducing-googles-satellite-embedding-dataset-31744c1f4650) [R1-011](https://www.tensorflow.org/datasets/catalog/bigearthnet) [R1-051](https://github.com/birdnet-team/BirdNET-Analyzer) [R1-070](https://registry.opendata.aws/maxar-open-data/) [R1-026](https://huggingface.co/datasets/DBD-research-group/BirdSet).

## Q5. Gaps a Mediterranean-wetland, herpetofauna/Odonata/vegetation-labelled set would fill

Gaps list (each from searches above; "in my searches" means absence was not exhaustively proven):
1. Wetland classes at habitat level: WorldCover reports lower accuracy for herbaceous wetland [R1-015](https://worldcover2021.esa.int/data/docs/WorldCover_PVR_V2.0.pdf); BigEarthNet labels come from CORINE, not field surveys [R1-008](https://bigearth.net/static/documents/Description_BigEarthNet-MM.pdf); Sen1Floods11 covers flood water only [R1-013](https://huggingface.co/datasets/harshinde/sen1floods11); Global Wetland Watch stresses that field validation is still needed and its inventory was in development in 2024 [R1-022](https://www.globalwetlandwatch.org/news/2024/towards-a-global-high-resolution-inventory-of-wetland-typologies/).
2. Transferability between Mediterranean-type wetlands: aquatic vegetation is the hardest class to transfer between Camargue and Champagne humide (F1 up to 85%) [R1-104](https://consensus.app/papers/details/fe4256f2f79158b388e4d2577cfe46ea/); most wetland vegetation studies are site-specific and single-date, and a nine-site temperate reference set needed about 400,000 samples [R1-103](https://consensus.app/papers/details/31ce74991d485d17a42cd7f70c5bbf9c/); Greek wetland mapping is a single-site case [R1-105](https://consensus.app/papers/details/6c082bf023a459f89a5e6cfe2bdeb5f1/).
3. Temporal series: PANGAEA states GEO-Bench does not fully incorporate the temporal dimension [R1-001](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.04204v1); annual Sentinel time series help aquatic vegetation classes [R1-104](https://consensus.app/papers/details/fe4256f2f79158b388e4d2577cfe46ea/); multi-date labels are costly [R1-109](https://consensus.app/papers/details/f976376e6491522399cdab3876882893/). A multi-season labelled series for one delta would be unusual.
4. Southern Europe and Türkiye coverage: BigEarthNet's ten countries include no Turkish area [R1-008](https://bigearth.net/static/documents/Description_BigEarthNet-MM.pdf); PANGAEA and others note geographic bias [R1-004](https://consensus.app/papers/details/c9ba28e94dd65684b5faeb66aa594a86/); the one Turkish wetland paper found validated water classification on five wetlands with EuroSAT and notes labelled data is costly [R1-106](https://consensus.app/papers/details/5d0dcc5030875eaaa71bb1322d966d0a/). R2 covers Turkish datasets in detail.
5. Amphibian and reptile acoustics: BirdSet is avian [R1-027](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.10380); AnuraSet is Neotropical [R1-030](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-023-02666-2); a 2026 anuran review calls out the lack of standardised large-scale anuran datasets [R1-100](https://consensus.app/papers/details/f69b334b0d655d36a36419362d44fd6a/); a 2026 study says open amphibian classifiers are largely absent [R1-101](https://consensus.app/papers/details/d8ecae71751c54a9918ec8fec81a3f78/); European green toad PAM works for presence and phenology but not abundance [R1-102](https://consensus.app/papers/details/88a279679d235f7cbc54d237887e6cb7/); Perch 2.0 includes amphibians in training but the benchmark evaluation sets are avian-led [R1-054](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.04665). No reptile acoustic benchmark was found.
6. Mediterranean acoustics with labels: the Cyprus set is large (5,200 h) but its abstract does not describe species-level annotation [R1-097](https://consensus.app/papers/details/44ec5eea11dc5a29b40e617ca0a6c46e/); European insect acoustics exist for Orthoptera/Cicadidae [R1-098](https://consensus.app/papers/details/e6347e9daeed52bba9624d8096482585/).
7. Odonata: image sets found are scraped (54,176 images from iNaturalist, Flickr, crowdsourcing and Bing for 256 species) or tiny (141 specimens) [R1-110](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/361096362_Odonata_identification_using_Customized_Convolutional_Neural_Networks) [R1-111](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.09.01.610694v1.full); no field-verified, georeferenced Odonata set linked to habitat labels was found.
8. Co-located multi-modal labels (Sentinel + drone + acoustic + vegetation + taxon) at one site: none found. This is the product's distinctive angle and also a risk, since no existing benchmark format covers it.
9. Licence gap: BirdNET is non-commercial by default, xeno-canto-derived sets exclude CC-ND, Maxar open data is CC BY-NC; a commercially clean, expert-verified labelled set could be useful to commercial teams (inference, no buyer evidence found) [R1-051](https://github.com/birdnet-team/BirdNET-Analyzer) [R1-026](https://huggingface.co/datasets/DBD-research-group/BirdSet) [R1-070](https://registry.opendata.aws/maxar-open-data/).
10. Robustness testing: EarthShift finds about 15-20% worse out-of-distribution performance across 8 GFMs, which is an argument for held-out regional test sets [R1-107](https://consensus.app/papers/details/a2f633807ef450d3bcd42b354c92ed7a/).

## Limits

- Abstract and landing-page level only; no purchase evidence from FM teams and no price for labelled ecology data was found.
- Vendor-blog prices (imagery, annotation, marketplaces) are indicative and sometimes inconsistent.
- Some licences (GEO-Bench components, PANGAEA components, WorldCover validation data, LUCAS, GWW, BEANS) were not confirmed.

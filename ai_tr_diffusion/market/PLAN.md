# Market research plan — two product ideas (2026-10-05)

Products under study
- P1 "Gediz Delta Mediterranean wetland benchmark": field-labelled validation/training data (Sentinel + drone imagery with vegetation/habitat labels, bioacoustic recordings with herpetofauna/bird labels, Odonata/herp imagery) sold/licensed to EO foundation-model teams, bioacoustic model teams, nature-MRV firms and Turkish public data programmes; open benchmark + commercial licence.
- P2 "ÇED biodiversity pre-assessment pipeline": polygon-in → sourced pre-screening report (protected-species likelihood, data gaps, survey priorities) for Turkish EIA (ÇED) consultancies, RES/GES/JES developers and lenders (IFC PS6 / EBRD critical-habitat screening); herpetofauna/Odonata expertise as differentiator.

Research questions (same five for each product)
1. Who are the buyers and who are the current suppliers? (named firms, public bodies, consortia)
2. Which products/tools do they use today, which are the most used, and what are their features, data inputs, outputs and formats?
3. How are they priced / what business models exist (subscription, per-report, licence, open+paid, grant-funded)?
4. What does the Turkish side look like (volume, regulation, named firms, workflows, fees, data sources)?
5. Gaps: what do the most-used products NOT do that P1/P2 could (regional validation, Turkish species, lender requirements, expert verification)?

Workstreams (Sonnet, parallel, ≤40 WebSearch each; Consensus for peer-reviewed benchmarks; summaries only)
- R1 P1 global: EO/bioacoustic benchmark datasets and who publishes/consumes them (GEO-Bench, PANGAEA, BigEarthNet, Sen1Floods11, WorldCover validation, LUCAS, BirdSet, AnuraSet, xeno-canto); model teams' validation needs (IBM/ESA TerraMind, Clay, Google AlphaEarth, Cornell/BirdNET, Google Perch); data marketplaces and licences (Source Cooperative, HuggingFace, Zenodo, GBIF, Planet); nature-MRV firms buying ground truth (Pachama, Chloris, Space Intelligence, NatureMetrics, BeZero); pricing signals.
- R2 P1 Türkiye + Mediterranean: Ulusal Veri Kütüphanesi / 2026–2030 action plan data items, TÜBİTAK UZAY, DKMP Nuh'un Gemisi, Sulak Alanlar GM, MWO/Tour du Valat wetland extent products and their validation partners, Turkish anuran/bird acoustic datasets, existing Turkish labelled EO datasets; who in TR would pay or co-fund.
- R3 P2 Türkiye: ÇED volume and sector mix (Bakanlık e-ÇED stats; RES/GES/JES in Ege), ÇED yeterlik firm list and the biodiversity workflow (flora-fauna raporu, who does it, typical fees, tools: QGIS/ArcGIS, TÜBİVES, Nuh'un Gemisi, IUCN, GBIF), regulation (ÇED Yönetmeliği eki, Tabiatı Koruma kanun taslağı), lender-driven demand (IFC PS6/EBRD PR6 critical habitat assessments in TR; which consultancies do them: ERM, WSP, AECOM, Ramboll, Turkish firms).
- R4 P2 global comparators: IBAT (features, pricing, report types), NatureServe, UK Biodiversity Net Gain tools/metric, EcoAssess-type AI screening, eDNA pre-screening services, Wildlife Acoustics/ecoacoustic survey products, EIA automation startups; what they charge and what they lack for Türkiye.

Synthesis (Opus): 4–6 distinct product models per product (purpose, buyer, inputs/outputs, must-have features, pricing model, MVP in 90 days, moat, main risk), then Fable's final ranking.

Deliverables: market/market_en.md (detailed, English, sourced), market/pazar_tr.md (simple Turkish: examples, product models with design sketches, ranking), market/sources_market.csv.
Budget: 4 Sonnet agents (~40 searches each, 160 total ≤ session cap 200) + 1 Opus synthesis; Fable writes only the ranking.
